Cross-check — published print against recomputation from the index {{ reconVerdict }}

Why this panel: the ECB publishes both the HICP index and its annual rate of change. Recomputing the rate from the index catches vintage drift, rebasing accidents and this board's own arithmetic — three failure modes, one panel. Both series come from the same publisher, so this is an internal consistency check, not an independent second opinion; the published print stays the source of record and nothing is averaged.
